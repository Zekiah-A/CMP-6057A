Assessment Guidance - Coursework
----------

### Submitting Your Work ###

Allow time for checking your work and ensure you read the guidance.

* After submitting, check you have submitted the correct document by opening the file.
* If you have submitted the wrong file, you can immediately submit a replacement by selecting 'Start New'. Ensure this is done with minimum delay – it will be assumed that work in the submission point after the due date is for marking.
* Markers will mark the most recent submission.
* Blackboard may flag work submitted after the original due-date as 'overdue', please disregard this if you have an agreed extension.

### Academic Integrity ###

By submitting this assessment, you are certifying that this is your own work, based on your own intellectual efforts; that all sources are correctly attributed; and that the contribution of any artificial intelligence software is fully acknowledged.
