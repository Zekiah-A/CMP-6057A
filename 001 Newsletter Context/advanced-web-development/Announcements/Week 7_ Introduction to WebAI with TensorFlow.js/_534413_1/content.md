I hope your work on Coursework 001 is going well.

From Week 7, we will begin the second half of the module, focusing on WebAI. The ability to integrate AI models and features into web applications is becoming an increasingly valuable skill for modern web developers.

Our first WebAI lecture will take place on Tuesday, 3 November, from 14:00 to 16:00 in the Data Science and AI Lab (Sci 0.59).

This session will introduce the foundations of WebAI using Google’s TensorFlow.js. It will prepare you for the following weeks, when we will explore pretrained models, WebAI applications and bounded AI-agent features.

I appreciate that many of you will be busy completing Coursework 001. However, I strongly recommend attending this lecture because it establishes the key concepts needed for the rest of the module and Coursework 002.

The lecture will be followed by a practical lab from 16:00 to 18:00 in the same room. Weekly lecture slides, lab exercises and assessment information will be posted on Blackboard. Please review the materials before each lecture and lab where possible.
